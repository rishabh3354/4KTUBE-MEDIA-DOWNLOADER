## Features

* Download a YouTube video or its audio in one click, as MP4, WEBM, MP3 and other formats.
* Download a whole playlist at once.
* Pick one of a video's dubbed audio tracks when it has several.
* Download subtitles and closed captions with the video.
* Sign in to your YouTube account to download videos that require it.
* Download several videos at the same time.
* Save the audio of any video as an MP3.
* Download from Facebook, Instagram, TikTok, Twitter, Twitch, Vimeo, Rumble, Odysee, Bitchute, Reddit, SoundCloud and 1400+ other sites.
* Play videos inside the app without ads.
* Search YouTube from the app, with results in your regional language.
* No ads, no trackers and no malware.
* The interface is translated into 35+ languages.

Downloads go up to 4K and 8K when the source has them, and regular updates add support for new sites.
